Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How we use cookies

fluentai.uk uses a minimal set of cookies. We categorise cookies as follows:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the website to function correctly, such as remembering your cookie consent choice. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use our website, such as which pages are most popular, so we can improve it. These are only set with your consent, where required.

This site does not use cookies for third-party advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. Managing cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these settings in the "options" or "preferences" menu of your browser. You can also delete cookies already stored on your device at any time. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

4. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons.
