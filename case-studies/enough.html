Supporting Survivors with AI

Enough

Enough is a non-profit organisation dedicated to supporting individuals affected by sexual violence. Their mission is to provide accessible, compassionate, and practical support to survivors — whenever and however they need it.

FluentAI partnered with Enough to design and deploy an AI-powered chatbot that offers immediate, empathetic support to users visiting their website.

The Challenge

Survivors of sexual violence often face barriers when seeking help:

  • Difficulty reaching out due to fear, stigma, or emotional distress
  • Limited availability of human support services
  • Need for immediate, private, and judgment-free guidance
  • Complex journeys requiring tailored signposting and next steps

Enough needed a solution that could:

  • Be available 24/7
  • Respond with empathy and sensitivity
  • Guide users through options without overwhelming them
  • Adapt to a wide range of user needs and communication styles

The Solution

FluentAI developed a highly tailored AI chatbot designed specifically for trauma-informed interaction. Key features included:

  • Compassion-first language model trained to respond with empathy and care
  • Dynamic conversation flows that adapt based on user input
  • Clear signposting to relevant support options and next steps
  • Simple, concise responses to reduce cognitive load during distress
  • Privacy-first design, allowing users to engage safely and anonymously

The Impact

Early feedback from users highlights the effectiveness of the solution:

“The tone and language was so understanding and compassionate.”
“It is so kind and really asks the right questions.”
“It offers a range of steps available for dealing with sexual assault.”
“Responses felt natural and was good at adapting to responses that weren't that simple questions.”
“I enjoyed how simple and concise the information was.”

Results

  • 24/7 availability of support for survivors
  • Improved accessibility for users hesitant to speak to a person initially
  • Consistent, high-quality responses delivered with empathy
  • Positive user feedback validating tone, clarity, and adaptability

Why It Matters

For organisations like Enough, the first interaction can be the hardest step for a survivor. FluentAI's chatbot helps bridge that gap — offering immediate, compassionate support when it's needed most.

This project demonstrates how AI, when designed responsibly, can play a powerful role in delivering sensitive, human-centred care at scale.
