Transforming Absence Management with AVA

The digital reporting line for unplanned workplace absences.

“AVA has fundamentally changed how we manage absence. What was once manual and reactive is now instant, structured, and insightful. We are also able to connect our employees to our benefits at the point of need and track how this is being used more effectively.”

The Challenge

Organisations across the UK are struggling with high levels of employee absence, inefficient reporting processes, and inconsistent data capture.

Traditional absence reporting methods — phone calls, voicemails, and manual logging — created several issues:

  • Delays in notifying managers
  • Incomplete or inconsistent absence data
  • Lack of real-time visibility for HR teams
  • Increased administrative burden
  • Poor employee experience, especially outside working hours

For organisations with large or distributed workforces, this resulted in operational disruption and limited ability to proactively manage absence trends — which in turn can often lead to employees going on longer-term absence because short-term absence patterns haven't been picked up.

The Solution

FluentAI partnered with AVA to design and implement an AI-powered absence reporting and management platform, centred around automation, accessibility, and real-time insights.

Key Features

WhatsApp-Based Reporting

Employees report sickness quickly and easily via WhatsApp.

AI-Powered Data Capture

Natural language processing extracts key details such as reason for absence, expected duration, and risk indicators.

Automated Notifications

Line managers and HR teams are instantly notified via email, Slack, and internal systems.

HR System Integration

Optional integrations with platforms such as BambooHR and HiBob.

Analytics & Reporting

All data feeds into the FluentAI analytics layer, providing real-time dashboards, trend analysis, and predictive insights.

Implementation

FluentAI delivered the AVA platform through a phased approach:

  • Discovery & Workflow Mapping
  • AI Model Configuration
  • System Integration
  • Pilot Programme
  • Full Deployment

The implementation required no complex infrastructure changes.

Results

Immediate impact:

  • 24/7 absence reporting
  • Faster notification times
  • Reduced administrative workload
60–80% Reduction in manual HR processing time
24/7 Absence reporting availability
↑ Higher employee engagement

Strategic benefits:

  • Better visibility of absence trends
  • Identification of repeat absence patterns
  • Data-driven HR decision-making

Example User Journey

  • Employee reports absence via WhatsApp
  • AI captures and structures the information
  • Manager receives instant notification
  • HR system updates automatically
  • Data appears in dashboards for analysis

Why It Matters

AVA transforms absence management from a reactive, manual process into a proactive, data-driven system. Organisations can reduce costs, improve visibility, enhance employee experience, and free up HR teams for higher-value work.
