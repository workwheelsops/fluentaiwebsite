How It Works

A structured, safeguarding-led approach to designing, deploying and improving your AI chatbot.

Our four-stage process

1

Discovery & Risk Assessment

We begin by understanding your operational workflows, safeguarding responsibilities and enquiry types.

2

Knowledge Structuring & Training

Policies, FAQs and escalation pathways are structured into a controlled knowledge framework.

3

Deployment

Chatbots are deployed via website or WhatsApp integration, fully branded and aligned to your tone of voice.

4

Reporting & Optimisation

Admin dashboards provide conversation visibility, analytics and performance tracking. Continuous optimisation ensures sustained value.
